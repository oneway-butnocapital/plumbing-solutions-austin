Garbage Disposal Repair in Austin, TX

Repair for humming, leaking, jammed, or foul-smelling garbage disposals.

Call (737) 300-9206 Ask a question

Older kitchens across Austin, Rollingwood, West Lake Hills, and Lost Creek often have original sink flanges, switches, and disposal mounts. Those fittings can loosen or corrode as the disposal motor and drain connections age inside the sink cabinet.

We repair units that jam after food waste, stop at the wall switch, leak around the mounting ring, or make a low humming sound. A humming, leaking, or non-starting disposal should not be left in use because a stalled motor can burn out and water under the sink can damage the cabinet base.

Humming motor and sink odor

Customers often tell us the disposal hums but will not grind, makes a grinding or rattling noise, or trips off after the switch is turned on. Others report water dripping from the bottom of the unit, a leak at the sink flange, or standing water on the disposal side of the sink.

A sour sink odor can come from food trapped around the splash guard, but it can also point to residue inside the grinding chamber. A disposal that will not turn on may have a reset issue, a switch problem, a wiring fault, or a failed motor. These symptoms can sound alike from the kitchen, yet the motor, mounting ring, drain connection, and electrical parts lead to different repair work.

Under-sink inspection

We inspect the disposal body, mounting assembly, sink flange, discharge tube, dishwasher connection, switch, and wiring under the cabinet. We test the reset button and use a meter to check the electrical supply before we decide whether the fault is mechanical or electrical.

A basic inspection and operational test typically takes 20 to 40 minutes. We can often clear an accessible jam, reset an overload, tighten a fitting, or replace an available splash guard or mounting part on the same visit. A failed motor, damaged mounting assembly, or unit requiring a matching replacement part may need a return visit after the part is obtained.

Motor age and cabinet access

The disposal motor and its age affect the scope of repair. An older unit with a worn grinding chamber, leaking lower seal, or damaged mounting ring may need more than a reset or adjustment. A model that uses a specific switch, stopper, or mounting component can also require an ordered part.

Cabinet access matters around the drain trap, dishwasher hose, electrical cord, and water shutoff. Tight shelving, a seized shutoff, corroded fasteners, or a disposal installed behind fixed plumbing adds work before the unit can be removed or reconnected. We assess those fittings at the sink so the repair matches the actual fault and access.

Model plate and sink cabinet

Check the model number on the label underneath or on the side of the disposal body if the label is visible. Tell us which kitchen sink has the issue and whether the unit hums, leaks, grinds, or does nothing at the switch.

Let us know whether water is running from the disposal, the sink flange, or the drain connection. Clear anything stored in front of the sink cabinet if access is limited by bins, shelves, or cleaning supplies.

Call Plumbing Solutions Austin at (737) 300-9206 with the model number and symptoms. A leak at the disposal flange can swell the cabinet floor, and repeated switching can worsen a stalled motor.
