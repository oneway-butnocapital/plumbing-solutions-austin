Accessibility

This site is built to be usable with a keyboard, a screen reader and at a large text size.

What has been done

Every page is one column of ordinary HTML with headings in order. Images carry alternative text. Links are links rather than scripted elements, so they work without JavaScript. Text and background colours were chosen to stay legible when the page is zoomed.

There is a skip link at the top of each page, and the main content is inside a landmark a screen reader can jump to.

Known gaps

The embedded map comes from Google and we do not control how it behaves with assistive technology. The address and the hours are written out in text above it for that reason, so nothing is only available inside the map.

What this page is not

This is a description of how the site was built. It is not a conformance claim against WCAG, the ADA or any other standard, and the site has not been audited against one. It is not legal advice.

If something is hard to use

Call (737) 300-9206 and say what you were trying to do. It will be fixed, and in the meantime somebody will give you the information over the phone.
